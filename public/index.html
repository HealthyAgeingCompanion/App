The Healthy Ageing Companion

An early proof of concept from the Oxford Longevity Project

Before you start: this gives general guidance drawn from Sir Muir Gray's healthy ageing work, not medical advice, and it's not a substitute for seeing your GP. Your name, age band and location personalise this conversation and help find local activities near you. Gender, height and weight are optional and only help tailor suggestions. Nothing you type here is stored on our servers, unless you choose a two-week check-in at the end, in which case we keep only your email address and a short summary of today's plan, purely to send that one follow-up.
